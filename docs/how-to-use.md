# The OpenIGCSE Handbook

A guide for the family: how every page works, how to use it day to day, and what to do when something looks wrong.
It describes the app as it stands after Phase 8 (November 2026). For first-time setup, see the
[Setup Guide](setup-guide.md).

# Chapter 1: How the app is put together

## 1.1 The address

The app lives at **https://openigcse.vercel.app**. It works in any modern browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on a
laptop, tablet or phone. Nothing needs installing, though you can add it to the home screen (see 2.5).

## 1.2 Two areas, one app

- **The learner area** is where studying happens: today's plan, lessons, papers, review and messages.
- **The parent area** is where the day is planned, papers are set and marked, and requests are answered. It always
  asks for the **parent PIN**, even on a device that is already signed in.

## 1.3 Where things are kept

Everything the household does (timetable, plans, answers, marks, messages, photos) is stored in one online database.
Any device that signs in sees the same up-to-date information. If a device breaks, nothing is lost.

## 1.4 Finding your way around

- **On a phone:** the main pages are in the tab bar at the bottom. Everything else is under **More**.
- **On a laptop or tablet:** the pages are listed in the sidebar on the left.
- **Top bar, on every page:** **Ask** (learner only, see 3.3), the bell for notifications, the sound switch and the
  light/dark switch.

## 1.5 Two promises the app keeps

1. **It never hides a failed save.** If something could not be saved, you see a red "Not saved" message with a
   **Try again** button. Nothing is silently lost.
2. **It never makes up syllabus content.** Every objective comes from the official Cambridge syllabus.

# Chapter 2: Signing in and devices

## 2.1 The parent account

The parent signs in at the sign-in page with an **email and password**, or asks for an **email link** and opens it on
the same device. One parent account looks after the household.

## 2.2 The parent PIN

The PIN (four to six digits) unlocks the parent area. Keep it private from the learner.

- **Five wrong tries in ten minutes** lock the parent area for ten minutes. Wait it out; that's the protection working.
- **To change it:** Parent area > **Settings** > **Guardian PIN**. Enter the current PIN and the new one.

## 2.3 The learner's device

The learner does not use an email. Their device is **paired** instead:

1. Parent: **Settings** > **Student devices** > name the device > **Generate pairing code**. A six-digit code appears,
   valid for a short time.
2. Learner's device: open the app, go to the **Pair** page (the sign-in page links to it) and type the code.
3. That device is now signed in as the learner and stays signed in.

**A lost or replaced device:** Settings > Student devices > **Revoke**. It is signed out the next time it opens the app.
Then pair the new one.

## 2.4 Safety limits on signing in

Too many wrong passwords, email links or pairing codes in a short time are refused with "Too many attempts. Try again
in N minutes". Wait the stated time. This stops strangers guessing.

## 2.5 Adding the app to the home screen

- **Chrome or Edge:** the browser menu > **Install app**.
- **iPhone or iPad:** **Share** > **Add to Home Screen**. On an iPhone, notifications only work this way.

## 2.6 Notifications

Press the bell, then turn notifications on and allow them when the browser asks. The parent hears about requests and
messages. If notifications are refused, the bell still lists everything inside the app. **Quiet hours** (Settings)
keep the parent's phone silent at night; the bell still collects everything.

# Chapter 3: The learner's pages

## 3.1 Today

**What it's for:** the day's work, period by period. This is the page to open first every morning.

**What you see:**
- The date, a greeting, and how many minutes are planned.
- **Today's periods:** the current period and when it ends.
- **Period by period:** each period's tasks. Each task shows the objective, the planned minutes, its status
  (Planned, In progress, Done, Skipped) and **Why this**, the reason it was chosen.
- **Study next:** a ranked list of what is most worth doing, with the reasons underneath.
- **The lesson card** for the chosen objective: a study timer, a lesson video if there is one, and a checkpoint question.

**How to use it:**
1. Press **Start** on a task when you begin. The timer runs.
2. Watch the lesson and do the work.
3. Answer the **checkpoint** if there is one: pick an option, then **Check answer**.
4. Wrong answer? Choose a reason under "Why did you get it wrong?" and press **Add to my notebook**. It comes back in
   Review.
5. Press **Finish** when done. The time is recorded.
6. Can't do it? Press **Skip** and choose a reason.
7. Use the up and down arrows to change the order within a period.
8. Lost? **I am stuck** tells the parent straight away. It costs nothing and never breaks the streak.

**No timetable yet?** Today shows the ranked "Study next" list instead, until the parent builds the day (4.6).

**No internet?** Today still opens with the last plan the device saw. Start, Finish, Skip and study time are held on
the device and listed in a yellow banner ("2 changes are held on this device"). They are sent by themselves when the
connection returns, or press **Send now**. Checkpoints and **I am stuck** need a connection.

## 3.2 Timetable

The learner's week: each day's blocks with their times and subjects. It's read-only; the parent edits it (4.6).

## 3.3 Ask (the button in the top bar)

**What it's for:** asking the parent for something without leaving the work.

1. Press **Ask**.
2. Choose one: a break, more time, swapping two periods, a day off, help, or checking work.
3. Add a note if you like, then **Send**.
4. "Your parent has been told" appears. Press **Back to work**.

The answer arrives on screen within seconds. An approved break shows a countdown, and the period timer waits until it
ends.

## 3.4 Assessments

**What it's for:** papers and quizzes the parent has set.

1. Open **Assessments** and choose the paper. ("Waiting for marks" means it is handed in and not marked yet.)
2. Press **Start**. The clock begins.
3. Type each answer. Use **Add a photo of your working** for written work. Answers save themselves every 10 seconds
   ("Saved at 10:43").
4. Need longer? **Ask for more time** sends a request for 20 more minutes.
5. Press **Hand in** and confirm. When the time runs out, the paper hands itself in.
6. After marking, open it again to see the marks and the parent's feedback.

## 3.5 Review

Two tabs:
- **Due for review:** objectives studied before that are due to be recalled now, timed so they come back just before
  they would be forgotten. A right answer pushes a card further out; a wrong one brings it back sooner.
- **Mistake notebook:** every mistake, from checkpoints and from marking, grouped by reason. Fix one and mark it resolved.

The number on each tab says how many are waiting. Clearing these is the quickest way to win marks back.

## 3.6 Inbox (Messages)

Chat with the parent: text, a photo or a voice note. It shows read receipts and when the other person is typing.

## 3.7 Evidence

Send work for the parent to check: give it a title, then attach a photo or PDF (**Take a photo** on a phone), or type
it. The parent approves it or asks for a correction.

## 3.8 Subjects, Plan and Progress

- **Subjects:** the syllabus for each enrolled subject, topic by topic.
- **Plan:** how the term is laid out, week by week.
- **Progress:** a syllabus map coloured by how well each part is known, minutes studied against the plan, and the next
  exam.

## 3.9 Trivia

Documentaries with questions, just for fun. Not counted towards progress.

# Chapter 4: The parent's pages

Open **Parent area** in the menu and enter the PIN.

## 4.1 Overview

The front page: new requests, work waiting to be checked and today's summary, all updating live.

## 4.2 Live

**What it's for:** seeing what the study device is doing right now and acting on it.

- **Study device:** Online or Offline, last seen (time and page), the current period, task and timer, today's planned
  minutes done, and whether the app is on screen.
- **Remote actions:** **Unlock 15 min**, **Unlock 30 min**, **Lock now**, **End the school day**, and a box to
  **Send to screen** a short message. (Real locking arrives with the lockdown app in Phase 9. In a browser it shows as
  a status only.)
- **Requests:** each ask from the learner appears the moment it's sent. Answer with one tap: for a break, **5 min**,
  **10 min**, **15 min** or **Not now**. Add a reply if you like. "No open requests" means everything is answered.

## 4.3 Inbox (Messages)

The household chat, the same as the learner's side.

## 4.4 Marking desk

**What it's for:** marking handed-in papers.

1. **To mark** lists papers waiting. Choose one.
2. Each answer appears beside the model answer and the marking guide.
3. Press the mark for each question (**0**, **1**, **2** and so on). Add a note for the learner if useful.
4. Any marks taken off create mistakes in the learner's notebook automatically. **Notebook reasons** lets you change
   the reason if the learner picked the wrong one.
5. Add **Overall feedback** if you like, then **Save marks**. "Marked: 2 of 2" confirms it, and the paper moves out of
   To mark.

The save is all or nothing: marks, mistakes and progress are recorded together, or nothing is and a "Not saved"
message says why.

## 4.5 Assessments

**What it's for:** setting quizzes, tests and past papers.

1. **Assessments** > create one, or open an existing draft.
2. Fill in **Title**, **Kind** (weekly quiz, topic test, mid-term, final, past paper), **Subject**, **Date** and
   **Minutes**. **Grade thresholds** are optional: type Cambridge's published percentages for a grade estimate.
3. Add questions: search the bank under **Add from the bank** and press **Add**, or **Auto-pick 10 weakest** to choose
   the learner's weakest objectives.
4. Press **Save**. Scheduling always uses what is saved.
5. Press **Schedule**. The paper goes onto that day's plan. Tick **Make the date an exam day** to replace that day's
   normal timetable.
6. **Delete** removes a paper that hasn't been sat.

## 4.6 Timetable

**What it's for:** drawing the school week. The learner's Today page is built from it.

- **Add a block:** choose a chip (a subject, **Revision**, **Break**, **Free study**, **Assessment**), then click the
  grid where it should go. On a phone, tap **Add** and pick a start time.
- **Change a block:** drag it to move it, or drag its bottom edge to resize it. On a phone, tap or press and hold it to
  change its day and times. With a keyboard, the arrow keys move a selected block, Shift plus arrows resize it, and
  Delete removes it.
- **Copy Monday to weekdays** fills Tuesday to Friday from Monday. **Clear week** empties the grid.
- **Build today's plan** turns today's blocks into the learner's tasks. Press it after changing today's timetable.
- **Weekly minutes** compares each subject against its allocation. **Daily load** warns when a day goes over the cap.
- **Calendar exceptions:** for one date that's different (a **Holiday**, **Sick day**, **Half day**, **Exam day**,
  **Ramadan schedule** or **Custom**), pick the date and type, add a reason, set the study minutes, decide whether to
  **Keep the streak**, and press **Add override**. This replaces the week for that date only.

## 4.7 Term planner

Spread each subject's topics across the weeks of the term and mark the assessment weeks. It refuses to save terms
whose dates overlap or are out of order, and says which.

## 4.8 Questions

The household's question bank. Write multiple-choice, short-answer, structured or calculation questions, with images
if needed. Each is tagged to a syllabus objective. A multiple-choice question on an objective becomes that lesson's
**checkpoint** on Today.

## 4.9 Progress

Coverage per subject, the assessment average, how well reviews are kept up, and a readiness estimate. The estimate is
a guide, not an official prediction.

## 4.10 Curriculum

The imported Cambridge syllabuses. You use it when the learner moves to a new syllabus edition: confirm the links
between old and new objectives, then press **Move progress** so earlier work carries over. See
[syllabus-pdfs.md](syllabus-pdfs.md).

## 4.11 Focus

Session length and allowed hours for the study device. They are enforced once the lockdown app arrives (Phase 9).

## 4.12 Trivia

Add documentaries (a YouTube video) and their questions.

## 4.13 Settings

- **Learner profile:** name, year group, candidate type and target exam series.
- **Household region:** country and time zone. "Today" is worked out in this time zone.
- **Guardian PIN:** change the PIN (2.2).
- **Student devices:** pair a device or revoke one (2.3).
- **Quiet hours:** when the parent's phone stays silent.
- **Household data:**
  - **Download household data** saves one file with every timetable, plan, mark, message and request, plus links to
    each photo and voice note (the links last seven days). The parent account must be signed in on that device.
  - **Delete household** removes the learner, every record, every file and every account, permanently. It needs the
    PIN and the word DELETE typed out. Download the data first.

## 4.14 Student view

**Student view** in the parent menu opens the learner's pages, to see what they see.

# Chapter 5: A normal school day

| When | Learner | Parent |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Before school | | Check **Live**. If today's tasks are empty, **Timetable** > **Build today's plan**. |
| Each period | **Today**: Start, study, checkpoint, Finish | Answer requests on **Live** or the phone notification |
| A break | **Ask** > break > Send, then wait for the answer | Tap **10 min** (or 5, 15, Not now) |
| A quiz | **Assessments** > Start > answer > Hand in | Schedule it the day before (4.5) |
| After school | Clear **Review** | **Marking desk**: mark anything handed in |

# Chapter 6: A normal week and term

- **Weekly:** set a quiz for Friday from **Auto-pick 10 weakest**. Mark it within a day or two so the review schedule
  stays accurate. Glance at **Progress**.
- **When a day is different:** add a calendar exception (4.6). Don't redraw the week.
- **Each term:** check the term's dates and topics in the **Term planner**.
- **Each year:** when the learner moves up, follow [syllabus-pdfs.md](syllabus-pdfs.md) and **Curriculum** (4.10).

# Chapter 7: Fixing problems

Work down the list for the symptom you see. Most fixes are "refresh, check, try again". If nothing here works, note
the page, the time and the exact message, and ask Ian.

## 7.1 First steps for anything

1. **Refresh the page** (pull down on a phone, or F5).
2. **Check the internet** on that device. Another website should open.
3. **Check the app itself:** open **https://openigcse.vercel.app/api/health**. `"ok":true` means the app and database
   are running. Anything else, or no answer, means the service is down. Wait ten minutes, then tell Ian.
4. **Sign out and back in.**

## 7.2 Signing in

| What you see | What it means | What to do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| "That email and password do not match an account" | Wrong email or password | Check for typos, or use the email link instead |
| "Too many attempts. Try again in N minutes" | The safety limit (2.4) | Wait the stated minutes |
| The email link opens on the wrong device or says it did not work | Links work once, on one device, for a short time | Request a new link on the device you want to use |
| "That code has expired or is not valid" when pairing | The pairing code is old or was already used | Generate a new code in Settings |
| The learner's device was signed out | The device was revoked | Pair it again (2.3) |
| "Sign-in is unavailable right now" | The sign-in check couldn't reach the database | Wait a minute and try again; then check 7.1 step 3 |

## 7.3 The parent area

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The PIN is refused | Check the digits. After five wrong tries, wait ten minutes (2.2) |
| PIN forgotten | Ask Ian; it can be reset in the database |
| "Unlock the parent area first" | Enter the PIN again; the unlock expires after a while |
| "Sign in with the guardian account to save" | This device only used the PIN. Sign in with the parent email, then try again |

## 7.4 Today and the plan

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| "No timetable for today yet" | Parent: draw today in Timetable, then **Build today's plan** |
| Today's tasks are wrong after changing the timetable | Parent: press **Build today's plan** again |
| The wrong day or date | Settings > Household region: check the time zone |
| A yellow banner, "changes are held on this device" | Normal offline behaviour. Reconnect, or press **Send now** |
| "Not saved when the connection came back" | The database refused that change. Read the reason and do it again by hand |
| "No checkpoint is written for this objective yet" | Normal. Add a multiple-choice question for that objective in **Questions** |

## 7.5 Saving anything

A red **"Not saved. …"** line with **Try again** means that change was **not** recorded.
1. Press **Try again**.
2. Still failing? Read the message: it usually says what is wrong (a missing field, a wrong PIN, no connection).
3. Check 7.1. Your typing is kept, so nothing needs re-entering.

## 7.6 Requests, messages and Live

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A request never arrives on Live | Refresh Live. Check that the learner saw "Your parent has been told" |
| Live says the device is Offline | The learner's device is off, asleep or without internet |
| No notifications | Turn them on from the bell (2.6). On an iPhone, add the app to the home screen first. Check quiet hours |

## 7.7 Papers and marking

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| The learner can't find a paper | It's still a draft. Parent: open it, **Save**, then **Schedule** |
| "Pick a date and save before scheduling" | Fill in **Date**, press **Save**, then **Schedule** |
| "Add at least one question and save before scheduling" | Add a question, **Save**, then **Schedule** |
| A paper says "Waiting for marks" | Parent: mark it on the **Marking desk** |
| A mark was entered wrongly | Open the paper on the Marking desk again, change the mark, **Save marks**. Every change is recorded in the audit log |

## 7.8 Videos

| What you see | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| A video won't play | Check the internet. Some videos can't be embedded by their owner; choose another in Trivia or the lesson |
| "Videos play through YouTube" under the player | Required by YouTube's terms. Nothing to do |

## 7.9 When to call Ian

- `/api/health` doesn't say `"ok":true` for more than ten minutes.
- The same "Not saved" message comes back after Try again, on a good connection.
- Anything shows another family's data (it never should).
- Before using **Delete household**.

The database is backed up every night, and a backup can be restored if something is deleted by mistake.

# Chapter 8: Words used in the app

- **Objective:** one line of the Cambridge syllabus, such as "the concept of scarcity".
- **Mastery:** how well an objective is known, from 0% to 100%. The target is usually 70%.
- **Checkpoint:** a quick multiple-choice question at the end of a lesson.
- **Review (spaced review):** bringing an objective back just before it would be forgotten.
- **Mistake notebook:** the list of mistakes to fix, with reasons.
- **Period / block:** a slot in the timetable.
- **Override / calendar exception:** a change to one date only.
- **Entry route / tier:** Core or Extended, where Cambridge offers both.
- **Exam series:** the exam session being aimed for, such as June 2028.
- **Pairing:** connecting the learner's device with a six-digit code.
- **Revoke:** cutting off a device.
- **Audit log:** a record of parent actions (unlocks, approvals, marks) that nobody can edit.
