# How to Use OpenIGCSE

OpenIGCSE has two areas in one app. The **learner area** is where study happens. The **parent area** is where you
plan, mark and keep an eye on the day; it always asks for the parent PIN. This guide describes the app after Phase 7
(November 2026).

On a phone, the main screens are in the tab bar at the bottom; everything else is under **More**. On a larger screen
they are in the sidebar.

# For the learner

## Your day: Today

**Today** shows the date, the current period and when the next one starts.

- Each period lists what to study. Press **Start** when you begin and **Finish** when you are done; the time is
  recorded. Press **Skip** if you cannot do it and choose why. Use the arrows to change the order inside a period.
- A lesson card shows the objective, a study timer and, when there is one, a checkpoint question. Answer it to see if
  you were right and why.
- Got it wrong? Choose **Add to my notebook** and pick a reason, so you can come back to it.
- **I am stuck** tells your parent straight away. It never affects your streak.
- If there is no timetable yet, Today shows a ranked list instead, with the reason for the order underneath.

**No connection?** Today still opens with the last plan this device saw. Start, Finish, Skip and study time are kept
on the device, listed in a yellow banner, and sent when you are back online. Checkpoints and "I am stuck" need a
connection.

## Asking for things

Use the hand icon at the top to ask your parent for a break, more time, a swap, a day off, help, or to check your
work. Their answer appears on your screen within seconds, for example a break countdown.

## Other screens

- **Timetable:** your week.
- **Review:** **Due for review** brings back things you studied before you forget them; **Mistake notebook** lists
  your mistakes by reason. Resolve one when you have fixed it.
- **Inbox (Messages):** chat with your parent. Send text, a photo or a voice note.
- **Assessments:** papers your parent has set. The clock starts when you open one, your answers save every
  10 seconds, and the paper hands itself in when time runs out. You can photograph your working.
- **Evidence:** send work for your parent to check: a title, a photo or PDF (or **Take a photo** on a phone), or type
  it in.
- **Subjects** and **Plan:** the syllabus and how the term is laid out.
- **Progress:** a syllabus map coloured by how well you know each part, minutes studied against the plan, and the next
  exam.
- **Trivia:** documentaries with questions, just for fun.

# For the parent

Open the parent area from **Parent area** in the menu and enter your PIN.

## Overview

New requests from the learner, work waiting for review and today's summary, all live.

## Live

What the study device is doing right now: online or not, the current period and task, the timer, and today's
progress. Requests can be answered here with one tap. Remote actions:

- **Unlock for 15 or 30 minutes**, **Lock now**, **End the school day**.
- **Show a message on the screen.**

**Later (Phase 9):** the lock is enforced by the lockdown app. In a browser it is shown as a status only.

## Inbox (Messages)

The household chat, with read receipts and a typing indicator.

## Marking desk

Each submitted paper, answer by answer, beside the model answer and marking guide. Every mark you take off needs a
reason; that mistake goes into the learner's notebook automatically. One **Save** records the marks, the mistakes and
the progress together, or nothing at all if it fails.

## Planning

- **Timetable:** draw the week (see the Setup Guide). Changes reach the learner's Today screen.
- **Term planner:** topics per week and assessment weeks.
- **Questions:** write your own questions (multiple choice, short answer, structured, calculation, with maths and
  diagrams), each tagged to a syllabus objective.
- **Assessments:** build a quiz or test from the question bank, or let it pick the learner's weakest objectives, then
  schedule it.
- **Trivia:** add documentaries and their questions.

## Progress

Coverage per subject, the assessment average, how well reviews are kept up, and a readiness estimate. The estimate is
a guide, not an official prediction.

## Curriculum

The imported syllabuses. Use it when moving to a new syllabus edition: confirm the links between old and new
objectives, then press **Move progress**.

## Settings

The learner's details, paired devices (generate a pairing code, or revoke a device), the PIN, and quiet hours for
notifications.

# Good habits

- Draw the timetable once, then adjust single days with an override (holiday, sick day, exam day) rather than
  redrawing the week.
- Mark papers within a day or two, so the review schedule stays accurate.
- Encourage the learner to use **I am stuck** and the notebook; neither costs them anything.
- Keep the PIN private, and revoke any device you no longer use.
